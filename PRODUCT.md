# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students, fresh graduates, and working professionals preparing for technical and general job interviews.

## Product Purpose

InterviewCoach helps people rehearse realistic mock interviews, receive multimodal feedback on their answers and presentation, and track improvement over time.

## Positioning

The product combines adaptive AI questioning with transcript, speech, and facial-signal analysis in one practice loop, so users can improve both what they say and how they deliver it.

## Operating Context

Users practice from a laptop or desktop with a camera and microphone, optionally upload a resume and job description, complete a guided question sequence, and return to history for score trends and recommendations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing web app supports JWT authentication, resume upload and parsing, tailored interview setup, adaptive follow-up questions, browser camera and microphone checks, speech transcription, multimodal answer analysis, and session analytics. Preserve current routes, API contracts, browser permissions, and graceful text-only fallback behavior.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is InterviewCoach. The interface should feel focused, candid, and constructive rather than gamified or corporate.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the working React/Vite frontend, Express backend, FastAPI AI service, and MongoDB integration. There are no approved external brand assets, testimonials, or visual references.

## Product Principles

- Make the next practice action obvious.
- Turn complex signals into understandable feedback.
- Treat preparation as a repeatable craft, not a one-time test.
- Be honest about device and analysis limitations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, touch-friendly controls, and a usable text-only path when camera or microphone access is unavailable.

# InterviewCoach Web App Direction Contract

## Scope and mode
Operate mode across the authenticated dashboard, interview, history, and auth entry points.

## THESIS
This is a practice desk for making the next interview answer stronger. It refuses the generic purple AI dashboard: progress, preparation, and feedback should feel like one focused working session.

## OWN-WORLD
The world uses a pale blue-grey canvas, ink navy structure, and a small set of purposeful colors: coral for the primary start action, teal for healthy/verified states, and saffron for attention and coaching notes. Surfaces are crisp and lightly elevated, with generous spacing, compact labels, and IBM Plex Sans display text paired with DM Sans body text. Cards frame real tasks and feedback, not decorative feature tiles.

## STORY
A returning user immediately sees where they are in preparation, starts a tailored practice session, and can understand recent performance without leaving the working context. During an interview, the question and answer remain dominant while device and speech status stay legible but subordinate. History turns scores into next-step coaching.

## FIRST VIEWPORT
The app shell has a slim sticky navigation with a visible practice/history split. The dashboard opens with a dark ink welcome band and a coral action path into setup, followed by a two-column workspace: the session form owns the left and recent progress owns the right. Auth screens use the same visual language but stay compact and calm.

## FORM
Practice desk / editorial operations surface, grounded candidate 5 from concept seed 5a7838db. The form is the main instrument; status colors and a tight spacing rhythm make the workflow scannable without flattening every control into a card.
